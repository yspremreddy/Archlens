## Identify

"Assists in developing an organizational understanding to managing
cybersecurity risk to systems, people, assets, data, and capabilities."
This function helps organizations understand their business context and
prioritize cybersecurity efforts accordingly.

## Protect

"Outlines appropriate safeguards to ensure delivery of critical
infrastructure services" and limits the potential impact of a
cybersecurity event, through access controls, staff training, and data
security measures.

## Detect

"Defines the appropriate activities to identify the occurrence of a
cybersecurity event" and enables timely discovery through anomaly
detection and continuous monitoring capabilities.

## Respond

"Includes appropriate activities to take action regarding a detected
cybersecurity incident" and supports containing the impact of a
potential incident through response planning, communications, and
mitigation activities.

## Recover

"Identifies appropriate activities to maintain plans for resilience and
restore capabilities impaired due to a cybersecurity incident," to
reduce the overall impact of the incident.
